Privacy notice

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What this website collects

This website does not use advertising cookies or trackers. It does not store the information you type in the request form on a server: the form only prepares a WhatsApp message in your browser.

WhatsApp

When you press “Continue on WhatsApp”, you are taken to WhatsApp. What you send there is handled by Captain Busara and by WhatsApp under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy. We use your details (date, hotel, number of people, name) only to answer your request and organise the tour.

Google Maps

The map is loaded from Google only after you click “Show map”. Google may then set cookies under its own policy.

Fonts and images

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Images are served from this website.

Analytics

If cookieless, privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible) is switched on, it counts visits and button clicks without cookies and without personal profiles.

Contact

Questions about your data: message Captain Busara on WhatsApp or call the number shown on the website.
